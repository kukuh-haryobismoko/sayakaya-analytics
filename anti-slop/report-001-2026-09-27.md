## Found during verification (not in the audit)

- **Mobile overflow in the top bar (already existed; my 44px change made it worse).** `.navbar-right` could not wrap: at 320px the page was 355px wide before, 389px after #4. Fixed with `flex-wrap: wrap`.
- **Overview fund-filter dropdown on phones (already existed).** `.align-left` reset `right` to `auto`, so the fixed sheet sized to its content past the edge. Fixed by including `.align-left` in the mobile rule.
- **Overview charts at 320px (already existed).** Grid items could not shrink below a canvas's pixel width, and canvases kept Chart.js's 300px default. Fixed with `.grid-2 > * { min-width: 0 }` and `.chart-wrap canvas { max-width: 100% }`.
- **Sticky top bar on phones.** It was already about 220px tall at 320px; with 44px controls it is about 300px. It no longer sticks under 900px, so it scrolls away with the page.
- **Fund share SQL.** `pct_of_total` now uses `SAFE_DIVIDE` (Node + Supabase) so an all-zero day can't raise divide-by-zero.

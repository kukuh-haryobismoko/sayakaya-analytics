What buyers do before they buy
Share of people who completed a buy within 7 days of first doing each action in September. Steps inside the buy flow itself are left out.
Completing the risk profile is the strongest signal outside the buy flow: 84.8% went on to buy, 5.3× the average. Opening a push notification sits below average (12.5%).
Read with care
These are habits of people who buy, not proof of what makes them buy. They are useful for choosing who to talk to, not for claiming a feature drives sales.

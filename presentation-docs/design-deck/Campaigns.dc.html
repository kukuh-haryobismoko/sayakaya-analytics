Revenue side works now — cost side is the real gap
Yesterday's new Campaign revenue section prices the fee earned on units a promo locked up. For the two promos with a real fund lock: THRCUAN earned ≈Rp 2.9 M in fee against ≈Rp 9.4 M in bonuses (94 users × Rp 100 K) — net ≈ −Rp 6.5 M. PINNACLETOP earned ≈Rp 2.4 M against ≈Rp 180 M (18 users × Rp 10 M) — net ≈ −Rp 177.6 M. campaigns.used_quota reads 0 on both completed promos, so we costed by investors × bonus_amount instead of pulling it automatically — that field needs fixing before ROI can run unattended. KIMFORE and the other no-lock promos still can't be priced this way; they'd need a buy-volume ROI, not a fee one.

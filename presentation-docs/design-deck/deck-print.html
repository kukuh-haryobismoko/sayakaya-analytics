03 — Acquisition, continued
Before they uninstalled: most never got past KYC
What the uninstall cohort did, ever, before their last session · Google Analytics, Jul 2026
Push didn't hold them
Of everyone who got a notification, 43% dismissed one and only 19% ever clicked one (1.3% click-through overall). In-app messages fared no better — dismissed as often as they were shown.
A promo didn't save the sale
Up to 14 people clicked into a promo screen at some point and still ended up in this cohort — a promo click alone isn't a retention signal.
Some left on purpose
17 people logged out manually and 4 cleared the app's local data before disappearing — a deliberate exit, not silent drift, for at least this small group.
Cohort is the July uninstall population traced in GA (~239 people reached by first_open in this trace, close to the 303 uninstalls on the Acquisition slide — GA doesn't join this to registration/KYC/buy in the warehouse, so these are correlated events, not a proven causal order). "Signed up" sums Google and email/phone success events as parallel paths; not deduplicated against each other.

For the two promos with a real fund lock: THRCUAN earned ≈Rp 2.9 M in fee against ≈Rp 9.4 M in bonuses (94 users × Rp 100 K) — net ≈ −Rp 6.5 M.

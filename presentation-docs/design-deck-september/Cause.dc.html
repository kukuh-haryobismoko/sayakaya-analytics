The headline +Rp 48.3 B net flow usually quoted for August is whole-platform. Strip out RAIZ and 39 institutional/corporate accounts and retail net flow was +Rp 1.1 B — barely positive, and this is what actually moved the account-level table below.

Retail buying was genuinely ordinary-sized and broad: Rp 10.2 B across 685 orders from 287 investors, against Rp 9.1 B in redemptions — both well below July's Rp 19.7 B / Rp 76.8 B. Institutional accounts are a separate story: just 4 of them did Rp 58.8 B in buys and Rp 11.6 B in redemptions, net +Rp 47.3 B — almost the entire platform-wide net flow number.

Buying again and staying invested are different things
February's cohort re-bought only 41% of the time by M+1 on the previous slide, but 97% are still net-positive here, they just haven't come back to buy more. Most cohorts hold 70-97% net-positive well past M+1.
Two things worth watching
January's M+6 total flips to −Rp 28.6 B even though 70% of the cohort is individually still net-positive, a handful of large redemptions outweighing everyone else, consistent with the concentration seen earlier. August's 33% M+1 is the lowest in the table and too fresh to explain yet.

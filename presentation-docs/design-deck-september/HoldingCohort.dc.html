Buying again and staying invested are different things
February's cohort re-bought only 41% of the time by M+1 on the previous slide, but 97% are still cumulatively net-positive here. Most cohorts hold 70-97% net-positive well past M+1, even the ones showing net redemptions that specific month.
January sold down every month after its M0 spike, and it's 6 accounts
January put in +Rp 204.7 B in its own first month, then net-redeemed in every single stretch since: M+1 through M+6 are all negative, −Rp 233.2 B combined. Just 6 of the 89 accounts drive nearly all of it, one alone moved +Rp 50 B in January and a large chunk of the M+6 redemption. Read the percentages, not the Rupiah swings, as representative of the 89.

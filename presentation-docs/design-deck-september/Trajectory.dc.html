The number nobody will spot on their own
Last month's big-cheque story is unwinding, not building — the average buy ticket fell from Rp 120.9 M in July to Rp 100.2 M in August to just Rp 7.8 M in September's first nine days. The median, which had halved twice into July, actually recovered to Rp 1.0 M this month. August's positive flow came from ordinary-sized buying spread across 290 investors, not a handful of large tickets.

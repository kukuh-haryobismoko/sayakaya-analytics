03 — Acquisition, continued
Before they uninstalled: how far this cohort got
Distinct users who ever fired each event · Google Analytics, Aug 2026 uninstall cohort
Push didn't hold them either
Of the 138 people who received a notification, 78% dismissed at least one and 20% ever clicked one. In-app messages barely reached this cohort at all — only 7 people ever saw one, too small a sample to read into.
A promo didn't save the sale
Up to 12 people clicked into a promo screen at some point and still ended up in this cohort — same finding as July, a promo click alone isn't a retention signal.
Some left on purpose
12 people logged out manually and 1 cleared the app's local data before disappearing — a deliberate exit for at least this small group, same pattern as July.
Cohort is the August uninstall population traced in GA. This month's export is marginal per-event distinct-user totals, not a per-user ordered trace like July's — so the step rates above read as "at least X people reached Y," not a strict sequential funnel (a person could finish KYC without a recorded register_success, for example). GA still doesn't join to registration/KYC/buy in the warehouse, so these remain correlated events, not a proven causal order.
03 — Acquisition
Referral went from 3% of signups to 52% in six weeks
Referral signups ÷ total registrations, by month
Two months flat at ~3%, then a step-change in August that's still climbing. Last meeting's cumulative "7.8% of 2026 signups" framing averaged a dead channel with a suddenly-hot one, hiding this. Something changed in early August — if the referral page renewal we've asked about since July shipped, this is the result. Worth confirming directly; September's 52% is only 52 people.
Still true: referral converts better
Across 2026, referral activates at 3.5× the rate and invests 2.2× more at the median than organic — real, though smaller than August's reported 4.6×/40×.
What to do with this
If referral is really working now, it's the highest-leverage lever on the board — 3% of signups two months ago, possibly the majority by October.

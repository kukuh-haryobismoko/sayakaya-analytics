AI INSIGHTS

AI drafts the weekly digest and flags unusual days

The figures come from the database; the AI only writes the sentences around them.

Weekly digest
One click writes a management summary: AUM, net flow, buys, sells and new users against the week before. Money values blurred here.
Anomaly scan
Flags days where AUM or buy volume moved outside the normal range (z-score = how many standard deviations from normal).
Business impact
Problems surface in the same week, not at month end. On 5 Nov the scan flagged AUM and buy volume.
Used by maureen and the analytics owner
Actual use of this page is not logged, so it is not counted as adoption.

CONTROL

Everyone sees only their own sections, and every action is logged

AI on company data needs guardrails. Here is what is in place today, and what is still open.

User list: one login per person with an access list per section (the blue chips). Emails blurred.
Access per section
Each login opens only its own screens; changes apply immediately.
Activity log
Logins, exports, Ask questions and sensitive views are recorded, so the company can answer who opened or exported what.
AI stays inside the data
Digest figures come from the database; Ask shows its SQL and states what the data does not show.
One USD rate across the platform
Per the analytics owner, aligned with Sinvest. Every report and export shows the rate and its date.
Open: releases still run by hand
Versions are released by one person's script. The automatic Google Cloud release exists but stays switched off until its secure login is finished.
Open: AI use is still narrow
Ask: 6 logged questions by one person. Digest and anomaly scan: use not measured.

NEXT

Where this goes next

What has worked at Sayakaya, and what the taskforce is looking at.

WHAT WORKED AT SAYAKAYA
1. One trusted data layer
Every team reads the same numbers.
2. AI on top for the routine work
Plain-English questions, a written weekly digest, an unusual-day scan.
3. Access and audit from day one
Per-section access and an activity log.
4. Cost watched like any other line
Cloud data bill down about 70% (estimate).
WHAT COMES NEXT

1. Finish CI/CD at Sayakaya, so releases run on Google Cloud, not one person’s computer.

2. Three more cloud-cost savings are identified. If all are done, the monthly data bill could fall from about Rp 0.37M to about Rp 84,000 (analyst estimate).

3. Happy to share the approach with other Group companies that are interested. Nothing is scoped yet.

Direction for discussion. Evidence: Sayakaya only.

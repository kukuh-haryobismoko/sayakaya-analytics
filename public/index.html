Getting started

This dashboard shows live data pulled directly from the database — there's nothing to configure, just pick a section from the menu on the left. A few things that apply everywhere:

  1. Date range — the "From / To" fields at the top of the page and the Apply button next to them control the time window for whichever dashboard you're looking at.
  2. Menu (sidebar) — click the ☰ button top-left to collapse it to icons-only (desktop) or open/close it (on phone/tablet). Click a section title (like "Dashboards") to fold that group away if you don't use it often.
  3. Theme — top-right: Auto follows your device's light/dark setting automatically, or pick Light/Dark to fix it.
  4. Exporting — wherever you see CSV or Excel buttons, that downloads exactly what's on screen as a file.
  5. Your account — your name is in the box under the logo; Password lets you change it, Log out signs you out.
  6. Language — top-right: switch between English and Bahasa Indonesia at any time; your choice is remembered.

Dashboards

📊 Overview

The single-page snapshot of the whole business: total assets under management, user counts, buy/sell volume, a transaction trend chart, fund breakdowns, and a map of where investors are located across Indonesia.

📈 AUM history

How total assets under management (and the platform's own revenue) have moved over time.

🎯 Performance

How each fund's price (NAV) has performed — pick a time window from 1 day up to 5 years and compare funds side by side.

🌱 Growth

Marketing and growth numbers: how well promo campaigns performed, who's referring the most new investors, and which funds people switch between most.

Investor

👤 Portfolio (PWC)

Look up one investor by their SID code, name, or email, then see exactly what funds they currently hold, how their money has grown over different periods, and their AUM history over time.

🧭 Portfolio Explorer (GS)

Same idea as Portfolio (PWC) — search for an investor and see their holdings — but from a second data source, organized by the investor's savings goals, and viewable as of any past date.

🛠️ Portfolio Explorer (Main)

Same as Portfolio (PWC) — same as-of-date snapshot feature — but sourced from a corrected daily table (mi_fee_logs.portfolio_fix) whose average buy price is weighted by unit instead of averaged per lot, fixing a cost-basis bug in the original portfolio_with_code pipeline.

🧾 Portfolio (TX)

Investor lookup whose average buy price is computed directly from the transaction ledger, not from portfolios.initial_price — only completed buy/SWITCH_IN/reinvestment/transfer_in transactions affect the average; sells never change it. Supports an as-of date (reconstructed purely from transactions up to that date; bonus holdings only show live, since bonus_portfolios has no history). Lets you uncheck individual fund rows (e.g. dust balances) before exporting.

📇 Portfolio (SInvest)

Same as Portfolio (TX), but every number is built from the KSEI/SInvest custodian feed (sinvest.trx_history) instead of the app's own transactions table — a second, independent source for cross-checking one investor's holdings and average buy price.

💎 HNWI

"High-Net-Worth Individuals" — the list of investors holding within a chosen AUM range (you set the min/max thresholds) as of a chosen date, including each investor's risk profile (risk level, investment priorities, risk tolerance), with a total-across-everyone summary and an export. The per-fund breakdown below defaults to that same investor list; applying its own Min/Max fund AUM filter switches it to generate its own list by fund holding amount instead, independent of the filter above.

Operations & Finance

🧮 Reconciliation

A daily check that the numbers line up across systems — use this to catch discrepancies before they become a real problem.

📇 SInvest transactions

Browse the raw KSEI/SInvest custodian feed (sinvest.trx_history) directly — the same source Reconciliation compares against, but row-by-row and filterable/exportable, for tracing a specific transaction or investor.

💰 Revenue (PWC) / 💹 Revenue (GS)

How much revenue/fees Sayakaya itself has earned, broken down by fund and investment manager, from each of the two data sources the platform tracks (PWC and GS).

⏳ User lifetime

The same fee revenue as Revenue (PWC), but answered per investor instead of per fund: what each one earns the platform, next to how long they've been with us — when they registered, when they first bought, and how long they've stayed invested. Click any investor for their month-by-month, fund-by-fund breakdown.

🎯 Campaign revenue

What each promo campaign earned back. A buy settled with a promo code locks those units up, and this estimates the management fee they earn while they stay invested — until the investor redeems early, or sells them after the holding period ends. Shown next to the campaign's bonus payout so you can see which promos paid for themselves.

🤝 Remisier sharing (GS) / (PWC)

Calculates how much revenue-share is owed to remisiers (referral partners) based on the AUM they've brought in, from each data source.

🧾 Remisier transactions

The underlying transaction-level detail behind the remisier sharing numbers, searchable and filterable.

🔮 Predict

Machine-learning forecasts: projected AUM and transaction volume, which investors are at risk of leaving (churn), and retention trends over time.

Tools

💬 Ask

Type a question in plain language — like "top 10 funds by AUM" — and get an answer with a chart, no technical knowledge needed. You can ask follow-up questions, exclude things by name, and request a specific chart type or color. Restricted columns (passwords, KYC fields) are always left out of the results — superusers can tick "Include restricted columns" and confirm their password to see them for one question.

🔍 Data explorer

Browse the underlying data tables directly with filters, for when you need to see the raw records themselves rather than a summarized dashboard.

🧪 SQL lab

For technical users: write and run your own read-only database query. Restricted columns (passwords, KYC fields) are always left out of the results — superusers can tick "Include restricted columns" and confirm their password to see them for one query.

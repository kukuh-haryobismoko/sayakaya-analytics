What this means: the core tables are clean (no duplicate users, no orphans at the last full check), but several date fields are unreliable for old users, so use status for "who is verified" and dates only for timing. Most pre-login GA activity cannot be tied to a person (88.7% of devices), so GA is good for trends and the DB is the source for exact counts.

Not checked this phase: email/notification logs (in Supabase, not BigQuery), refreshed freshness of the scheduled copies of `main.*`, `kalcer`, `dormant` and `ml.churn_features` datasets.
